# HIT4HIT™ Music Submission Guidelines

## Submit Your Music

Thank you for your interest in HIT4HIT™. We are looking for talented artists ready to showcase their music and compete on the Road To The Crown.

Please follow the submission guidelines below to ensure your music is reviewed properly.

---

# Artist Information

Please include:

- Artist Name / Stage Name
- Legal Name (optional)
- Contact Email
- Phone Number
- City / State
- Social Media Links
- Biography (short introduction)
- Artist Photo / Press Image

---

# Music Requirements

Submit:

✅ Original music only  
✅ High-quality audio file  
✅ Clean version recommended  
✅ Completed songs preferred  
✅ Proper song title and artist credits  

Accepted formats:

- MP3
- WAV
- M4A

# Battle Submission

For HIT4HIT™ Battles, artists should submit:

- Best performance track
- Battle-ready music
- Performance video (if available)
- Artist photo
- Social media handles

---

# Promotional Rights

By submitting music, artists authorize HIT4HIT™ / STV Radiomeltdown to review submitted material and use approved promotional content for marketing, artist features, event promotion, and related media.

Artist ownership remains with the artist.
