# HIT FOR HIT™ / STV Radiomeltdown
## Artist Promotion, Media Release & Marketing Authorization Agreement

This Artist Promotion, Media Release & Marketing Authorization Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between HIT FOR HIT™ / STV Radiomeltdown ("Company") and the undersigned Artist ("Artist").

### 1. Purpose
The Artist authorizes the Company to promote the Artist through its radio station, website, social media channels, live events, digital platforms, marketing campaigns, and future promotional opportunities.

### 2. Promotional License
The Artist grants the Company a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free, revocable license to use the Artist's approved promotional materials for marketing purposes, including:

- Artist name and stage name
- Photographs and promotional images
- Logos and branding supplied by the Artist
- Album and single artwork
- Music excerpts for promotional purposes
- Music videos, interviews, and performance clips
- Biography and press materials

This license is granted solely for promotional and marketing purposes and does not transfer ownership of the Artist's intellectual property.

### 3. Social Media Authorization
The Artist authorizes the Company to:

- Create promotional graphics using submitted photos.
- Tag the Artist's official social media accounts.
- Share, repost, and cross-promote content published by the Artist.
- Schedule promotional posts across Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X (Twitter), YouTube, Snapchat, Threads, LinkedIn, and future social platforms.
- Include the Artist in promotional flyers, event graphics, advertisements, livestreams, and digital campaigns.

### 4. Website & Mobile Promotion
The Artist authorizes the Company to display approved promotional materials on:

- Radiomeltdown.org
- STV Radiomeltdown websites
- HIT FOR HIT™ websites
- Artist profile pages
- Digital press kits
- Promotional landing pages
- Mobile-friendly promotional pages
- QR code campaigns

### 5. Radio & Streaming Authorization
The Artist grants permission for approved promotional materials to be featured during radio programming, livestreams, podcasts, interviews, countdowns, promotional announcements, and related broadcasts.

### 6. Ownership
The Artist retains full ownership of all copyrights, trademarks, music, recordings, photographs, logos, and other intellectual property. Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership to the Company.

### 7. Artist Responsibilities
The Artist represents that:

- All submitted materials are owned by the Artist or used with proper permission.
- The Artist has authority to grant this promotional license.
- Submitted materials do not knowingly infringe on another person's rights.

### 8. No Guarantee
The Company agrees to promote the Artist in good faith but does not guarantee:

- Airplay
- Bookings
- Record deals
- Streaming numbers
- Followers
- Sales
- Revenue
- Contest wins
- Media coverage

### 9. Removal of Content
The Artist may request removal of future promotional content by submitting written notice. Previously distributed broadcasts, printed materials, archived content, and third-party shares may not be removable.

### 10. Compensation
Unless stated in a separate written agreement, this promotional relationship does not create an employment, management, recording, publishing, or agency relationship.

### 11. Release of Liability
The Artist releases HIT FOR HIT™, STV Radiomeltdown, its owners, employees, volunteers, contractors, sponsors, affiliates, and partners from claims arising solely from the authorized promotional use of materials covered by this Agreement.

### 12. Governing Law
