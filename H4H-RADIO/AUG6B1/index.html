How It Works

This isn't a concert. It's a battle.

HIT FOR HIT is built around one idea: the crowd is the judge. Here's the format in plain terms.

01
The Format
Artists battle head-to-head through their music — original rounds, performed live, back to back, in front of a room that reacts in real time.
02
Who's Up
The lineup is announced ahead of the event, so you know exactly who you're coming to see before doors open.
03
The Judging
The crowd votes — and every artist gets an equal shot, regardless of when in the night they perform.
04
The Recap Mix
Before the final vote, the DJ runs a live recap mix — a quick-cut reminder of every round — so the crowd votes with the whole night fresh in mind.
05
The Crown
Every event winner advances toward a season finale — one artist walks away with the crown.
