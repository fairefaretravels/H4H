HIT FOR HIT: The Battle for the Crown Begins

BY THE STATIC  ·  4 MIN READ

The stage is set for HIT FOR HIT, a song-for-song competition where artists go head-to-head for recognition, respect, and the ultimate crown. Powered by STV Radiomeltdown, the event brings together rising voices ready to prove their talent one round at a time.

"Unlike a traditional showcase, HIT FOR HIT puts the spotlight on performance, creativity, and crowd impact."

— THE STATIC ON WHAT SETS H4H APART

Artists must defend their records. They challenge their opponents round after round, and deliver moments built to leave a lasting impression on the crowd in the room.

From the qualifiers to the final crowning event, every battle moves one step closer to finding the next King and Queen of HIT FOR HIT. Fans will decide which artists rise, which songs stand tall, and who earns the crown.

The competition is more than a battle — it's a celebration of independent artists, music culture, and the power of the next generation of talent.
